## Hard Future-Date Limitation

The trip is November 1-9, 2026. Many restaurants, bars, ferry schedules, transit schedules, NHL games, and seasonal attraction hours are not guaranteed this far ahead. The dashboard therefore records current operating status and current published hours, then flags items that must be rechecked before travel.
