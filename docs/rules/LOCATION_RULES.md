# Location Rules

Include locations only when they fit at least one of these:

- Strong geographic fit.
- Strong food/coffee/cocktail value.
- Important local or first-time visitor experience.
- Rainy-day utility.
- Coffee bean shopping value.

Exclude or mark optional when:

- Too far from the route.
- Poor budget value.
- Duplicative branch or category.
- Reservation friction creates unrealistic pacing.
- Operating status or hours cannot be verified.
