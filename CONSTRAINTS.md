# Constraints

- Target activity budget: $800 USD.
- Absolute activity ceiling: $900 USD for optional splurges only.
- Budget excludes airfare and hotels.
- Budget includes local transportation, Amtrak, food, cocktails, entrance fees, souvenirs, and coffee beans.
- Coffee beans are capped at $60 total.
- Walking and public transit are preferred.
- Rideshare is emergency backup only.
- Avoid permanently closed businesses.
- Avoid duplicate branches unless the branch is strategically useful.
- Avoid unrealistic days and excessive backtracking.
- Recheck all future-dated hours, fares, menus, and schedules before travel.
