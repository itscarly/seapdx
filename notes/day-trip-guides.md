## Day Trip 1: Cannon Beach & Haystack Rock (Friday, November 6, 2026)

### Overview
A full-day solo trip via public transit to Oregon's most iconic sea stack and tide pools. Total round-trip time: ~10-12 hours.

### Getting There

**Booking the Ticket**
- **Transit operator**: POINT NorthWest (Amtrak Thruway partner)
- **Route**: Portland Union Station ↔ Cannon Beach
- **Booking options**:
  - Online: www.oregon-point.com/routes/northwest (book in advance)
  - Phone: 1-800-872-7245 (Amtrak reservations)
  - In-person: Amtrak kiosks at Portland Union Station
- **Fare**: $12–$30 one-way (budget $18 as middle estimate)
- **Confirmation**: You'll receive a booking number; save this for check-in

**Trip to Union Station**
- **From Hotel Vance**: Walk or take MAX light rail to Portland Union Station (800 NW 6th Ave)
- **Time**: ~25-30 minutes from Hotel Vance
- **Arrival recommendation**: Arrive by 8:00 AM for the 8:28 AM westbound departure
- **What to bring**: ID, boarding pass (if printed), water, snacks for the bus

**The Bus Ride**
- **Departure time**: 8:28 AM from Portland Union Station
- **Arrival time**: ~10:46 AM at Cannon Beach (1170 S Hemlock St, downtown)
- **Duration**: 2 hours 18 minutes
- **Amenities**: Restroom on board, wifi, power outlets, wheelchair accessible
- **What to expect**: Scenic ride through Columbia River Gorge on the way west; mostly full coach seating

### What to Do at Cannon Beach

**Haystack Rock** (main activity)
- **Cost**: Free (public land, open 24/7)
- **What to see**:
  - 235-foot sea stack, the most photographed landmark on the Oregon coast
  - Best viewed from the beach at Tolovana Beach State Recreation Site
  - Tide pools around the base (low tide only — see below)
  - Surrounding beach, coastal town atmosphere
- **Timing**:
  - Arrive ~10:50 AM, giving you a full day until an afternoon return bus
  - Budget 3-4 hours for core activities: walks, photos, lunch

**Tide Pool Timing (CRITICAL)**
- **Why it matters**: Tide pools are only accessible at low tide; bring NOAA tide tables for Nov 6, 2026
- **How to check**:
  - NOAA Tides: www.noaa.gov/weather/marine or tide-forecast.com
  - Enter location: "Cannon Beach, Oregon"
  - Look for low tide times on Nov 6 in the 10 AM–3 PM window
  - Plan your beach walk to arrive 1-2 hours before low tide
- **Safety**: Lifeguards and patrol trucks monitor the area; stay off exposed rocks to protect tide-pool ecosystems
- **Accessibility**: Tide pools are in the rocks around the base of Haystack Rock; mostly flat access but wet/slippery surfaces

**Cannon Beach Town**
- **Main street**: S Hemlock St, packed with local shops, galleries, and casual eateries
- **Lunch options**:
  - Casual cafes for quick service (priority: fast turnaround for return bus)
  - Local breweries with food (e.g., Newman French Bakery for pastries)
  - Fish & chips, tacos, or crepes from vendor carts
  - Budget: $20-25 for lunch + drinks
- **Other activities** (if time):
  - Cannon Beach History Center (small museum, ~30 min)
  - Walk the town galleries or boutiques
  - Relax on the beach sand

### Returning to Portland

**Return Bus**
- **CRITICAL**: Exact return departure time must be verified before your trip
  - One data point found: 5:55 PM Astoria departure → 9:00 PM Portland arrival
  - Cannon Beach departure is earlier than Astoria
  - Call 1-800-872-7245 or check www.oregon-point.com/routes/northwest for Nov 6 schedule
- **Recommendation**: Plan to leave Cannon Beach by 4:15 PM to catch the bus with a safe buffer
- **Return to Union Station**: ~6:00–9:00 PM (depending on exact bus time)
- **From Union Station to Hotel Vance**: ~30 min by MAX or rideshare

### What to Pack

- **Weather**: Coastal conditions; layers essential (wind, cool temps, possible mist from spray)
  - Fleece or sweater
  - Windbreaker or rain jacket
  - Jeans or hiking pants
- **Footwear**: Waterproof or water-ready shoes (tide pools = wet feet)
- **Essentials**:
  - Water bottle (refillable)
  - Snacks or protein bar for the bus ride
  - Phone + portable charger (bus has outlets, but backup helps)
  - Camera for Haystack Rock photos
  - Sunscreen (reflection off water and sand)
- **Comfort**: Sunglasses, hat/visor, small backpack or crossbody bag

### Tips

1. **Book early**: POINT NorthWest can sell out on weekends; book at least 3-5 days ahead
2. **Tide tables are non-negotiable**: Checking low tide is as important as the bus schedule
3. **Keep time tight**: With a 10-12 hour day trip, stay organized on timing so you don't miss the return bus
4. **Cash preferred**: Many Cannon Beach vendors prefer cash
5. **No car? No problem**: The bus service is reliable and affordable; solo transit is the smart play here

## Day Trip 2: Multnomah Falls & Vista House (Sunday, November 8, 2026)

### Overview
A half-day to full-day trip via public transit to the iconic 620-foot waterfall and historic viewpoint. Total round-trip: ~6-8 hours. This is a lighter day-trip option compared to Cannon Beach, perfect for the day before your flight.

### Getting There

**Booking the Ticket**
- **Transit operator**: Columbia Area Transit (CAT) / Columbia Gorge Express (CGX)
- **Route**: Gateway Transit Center (Portland) → Multnomah Falls area
- **Booking**:
  - Online: www.ridecatbus.org/columbia-gorge-express
  - Phone: Contact information on the CAT website
  - In-person: Gateway Transit Center, 10500 NE Brazee St, Portland
- **Fare**: $5–$10 one-way (estimate $8, pending Nov 2026 pricing)
- **Service**: Year-round (unlike older seasonal patterns); winter schedule may have fewer trips than summer

**Important Note on November Schedule**
- CGX now runs year-round, 7 days/week
- However, **exact November frequency and times must be verified** at www.ridecatbus.org before your trip
- Winter (fall/winter) schedules often run fewer round-trips; the summer schedule can have up to 8/day

**Trip to Gateway Transit Center**
- **From Hotel Vance**: Take MAX light rail or rideshare to Gateway Transit Center (10500 NE Brazee St)
- **Time**: ~30 min from Hotel Vance
- **Arrival recommendation**: Arrive 15-20 minutes early
- **What to bring**: ID, boarding pass (if printed), water, snacks

**The Bus Ride**
- **Estimated departure**: 9:00 AM from Gateway Transit Center
- **Estimated arrival**: ~10:00 AM at Multnomah Falls
- **Duration**: ~45–60 minutes
- **Amenities**: Basic transit bus (check CAT website for specifics)
- **What to expect**: Scenic route through the Columbia River Gorge; some elevation change

### What to Do at Multnomah Falls

**Multnomah Falls** (main waterfall)
- **Cost**: Free (public access, no admission fee)
- **What to see**:
  - 620-foot main waterfall (tallest in Oregon)
  - Benson Bridge trail loop (moderate stairs and switchbacks)
  - Upper Falls trail (optional hike, steeper)
  - Surrounding gorge views and forest
- **Best photos**: From Benson Bridge for the classic full-waterfall shot with bridge in foreground
- **Duration**: 
  - Core walk (main viewpoint + Benson Bridge loop): 60–90 minutes
  - Extended hike to top: 2–3 hours (optional)
- **Timing**:
  - Arrive ~10:00 AM, giving 3–4 hours before an early-afternoon return bus
  - Leave by 2:00–2:30 PM to catch the return bus with a safety margin
- **Difficulty**: Easy to Moderate (stairs on Benson Bridge; if you skip the upper trail, it's mostly flat)
- **Weather note**: 
  - Falls are stunning in rain (more water flow)
  - Vista House view is weather-dependent
  - Common wind gusts; dress in layers

**Vista House** (nearby viewpoint)
- **What to see**: Historic 360-degree viewpoint overlooking the Columbia River Gorge
- **Cost**: Free (Oregon State Parks, free entry)
- **Duration**: 30–45 minutes
- **Access**: Short drive from Multnomah Falls (part of the scenic highway)
- **Tip**: Plan this as a shorter add-on after Multnomah Falls if time permits
- **Note**: Very windy; bring a windbreaker or hold your hat

**On-Site Amenities**
- **Restaurant**: Multnomah Falls Lodge (on-site, casual dining)
- **Gift shop**: Souvenir stand
- **Restrooms**: Available
- **No parking fee**: Free parking at the site

### Returning to Portland

**Return Bus**
- **CRITICAL**: Exact return departure time must be verified before your trip
- **Estimated**: Mid-afternoon (2:00–3:00 PM), but call www.ridecatbus.org or the CAT phone line to confirm
- **Return to Gateway Transit Center**: ~3:00–4:00 PM
- **From Gateway Transit Center to Hotel Vance**: ~30 min by MAX or rideshare
- **Back at hotel**: ~4:30–5:00 PM

Since this is a lighter day trip, you have evening time for Cartopia food carts:
- **Location**: SE Alder near SE 10th Ave (SE Hawthorne)
- **Transit from hotel**: ~25-30 min by MAX or rideshare
- **Cost**: $25–35 for casual food cart meal
- **Options**: Pad Thai, Korean BBQ, wood-fired pizza, or whatever looks fresh
- **Payment**: Bring cash; not all carts take cards

### What to Pack

- **Weather**: Mountain/gorge climate; cool and frequently windy
  - Layered fleece or sweater
  - Wind-resistant jacket (essential)
  - Comfortable hiking shoes or trail shoes (can be damp from spray)
- **Essentials**:
  - Water bottle (the hike up involves elevation)
  - Snacks or energy bar
  - Phone + charger (bus may not have outlets)
  - Camera for waterfall and gorge photos
  - Sunscreen (reflection off water and rock)
- **Optional**:
  - Hiking poles (if planning the full upper trail hike)
  - Small backpack or crossbody bag
  - Windproof hat

### Tips

1. **Verify the schedule early**: Call CAT or check their website at least 3-5 days out to confirm Nov 2026 CGX times
2. **Plan your timeline tight**: Morning departure at 9 AM means you need to leave Hotel Vance by 8 AM
3. **Weather is unpredictable**: Even if the forecast shows rain, Multnomah Falls is worth the visit (looks more dramatic with water flow)
4. **Upper Falls hike is optional**: The main waterfall and Benson Bridge loop are the core; the extra upper trail adds 60–90 min and steeper effort
5. **Vista House is windy**: If you go, brace for serious wind gusts; the view is worth it but dress accordingly
6. **This is a lighter day**: Unlike Cannon Beach, Multnomah Falls is a half-day trip; plan an evening meal or activity after returning

## Final Notes

Both of these day trips are doable solo via public transit and showcase the best of the Pacific Northwest:
- **Cannon Beach** is best for a full-day immersion in coastal scenery and tide pools
