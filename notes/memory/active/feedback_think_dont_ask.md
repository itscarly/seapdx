The user explicitly said: "if theres a 2hr gap, what will i do during that time? you need to help me organize, plan and think right? this is what you're supposed to do."

When there is a time gap, my job is to:
- Figure out what realistically fits (travel time + activity duration + buffer)
- Choose specific venues that match the user's stated goal (meet young men — college guys, athletes, young professionals)
- Verify geographic feasibility (within 15 min walk or 30 min transit of the next activity)
- Propose a complete, concrete plan

**Why:** I was asking the user what they wanted to do during gaps instead of doing the thinking myself. That transfers work back to them, which defeats the purpose.

**How to apply:** Never present a gap and ask "what should I put here?" Do the research, pick a specific venue, verify it fits the time and geography, then propose it. The user can redirect if needed — that is a much better use of their time than doing the planning from scratch.
